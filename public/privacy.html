Privacy & analytics

This portfolio uses GoatCounter to understand how many visits it receives and which projects and links people use. It records visit counts, referring website origins, approximate locations, browser and device information, and named interactions such as opening a project preview or clicking a contact link.

Analytics does not use tracking cookies or persistent visitor identifiers. It does not record your screen, form contents, name, or email address. IP addresses and browser information are processed temporarily by GoatCounter to estimate unique visits; your name and identity are not shown to the site owner. Counts cannot reliably identify one person across devices.
